Button / jog / shuttle mapping

mod_* actions only take effect while that button is held down: the jog wheel adjusts that parameter instead of VFO frequency for as long as it's held. band_up/ band_down/shuttle's cycle_presets step through whichever presets have their "RigDial" box checked on the Frequencies tab, in order. ptt keys the transmitter directly while held -- same caution as the Radio tab's Key Tx button.
