Radio Control

Direct rig control via Hamlib's rigctld daemon -- separate from WSJT-X's own rig connection (its UDP API has no frequency/mode/PTT control at all). Needs rigctld running and reachable at the host/port configured in app.py (default 127.0.0.1:4532).
